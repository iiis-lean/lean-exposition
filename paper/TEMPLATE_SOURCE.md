All bundled `.sty` and `.bst` files are unmodified. Original example TeX and bibliography files are in `official-template/` for reference, not an alternative manuscript. The manuscript is `main.tex`.

The guidelines currently specify at most 9 main-text pages at submission and 10 at rebuttal/camera-ready. References and appendices are excluded. An AI use statement is required; ethics and reproducibility statements are recommended. Recheck the official guidelines before submission.
